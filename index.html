Enter each answer the hider gives. The map keeps only the area that fits every clue and dims the rest.

Canton uses the canton containing your point. District uses the district containing your point (not Google-verified; some cantons have none, which is a valid null answer). Nearest station uses the station closest to your point. Same-length station name counts characters exactly as Google Maps writes your nearest station's name, including spaces and hyphens. Transit line asks whether the hider's line stops at your nearest station: yes keeps every station reachable by any line that also calls there, no cuts it.

Compares the hider's distance with yours. Place categories use the places currently shown, so the review filter applies.

The radius is measured from you, matching the rulebook. Named keeps the ground inside your radius that is nearer to that place than to any other in range. Not in range removes your whole radius. Metro lines are not included, since the app holds no metro line data.

Marks the hider's hiding zone as confirmed. The possible area collapses to that station's zone at the current hiding-zone radius.
