=== AIRPORT GOOGLE FLIGHTS AUDIT ===

Manual audit, 2026-08-03, corrected 2026-08-06. Cross-checked the 17 entries
produced by the 100-review proxy against Google Flights and Google Maps.

The rule is satisfied when Google Flights displays a flight to or from the
airport. It does not require year-round or scheduled service, so seasonal and
charter flights count. This is a manual/web pass because Google Flights has no
API. After a fresh Places sweep, repeat the check and update
GOOGLE_FLIGHTS_AIRPORTS in scripts/filter_places.py.

Google Flights-visible (kept): 5
No Google Flights flight (removed): 12

--- GOOGLE FLIGHTS-VISIBLE: kept ---
  - Bern Airport - Google Flights displays seasonal Helvetic flights from
    BRN, including Palma, Heraklion, Monastir, Kos and Djerba.
  - Geneva Airport - major international hub, with flights displayed
    year-round.
  - Sion Airport - Google Flights displays a seasonal weekly Helvetic flight
    between SIR and Palma. Its charter classification does not disqualify it.
  - St. Gallen-Altenrhein Airport - Google Flights displays Peoples
    Viennaline flights between ACH and Vienna.
  - Zurich Airport - major international hub, with flights displayed
    year-round.

--- NO FLIGHT DISPLAYED BY GOOGLE FLIGHTS: removed ---
  - Air Ballonteam Stefan Zeberli GmbH - a hot-air-balloon touring company
    near St. Margrethen, not an airfield at all.
  - Airfield Langenthal - small general-aviation grass strip.
  - Aerodrome Epagny - general aviation and gliding.
  - Birrfeld Airport - general aviation and flight school.
  - Emmen Airport - Swiss Air Force base, with no civilian passenger flight
    displayed.
  - Engadin Airport (Samedan) - general aviation and glacier flights; Google
    Flights directs travellers to other airports.
  - Flugplatz Speck Fehraltorf - general-aviation grass strip; Google Flights
    treats Fehraltorf as a municipality and suggests Zurich Airport.
  - Flugplatz Wangen-Lachen - general-aviation strip.
  - Grenchen Airport - general aviation and flight school.
  - Lausanne Airport (La Blecherette) - general aviation.
  - Lugano Airport - general and business aviation; Google Flights' generic
    Lugano city pages use nearby airports, not a flight from LUG.
  - Luzern-Beromuenster Airport - general aviation.

--- METHOD ---
For each airport, search Google Flights by airport name and IATA/ICAO code and
cross-reference its Google Maps pin. A positive result must identify the
airport itself in the itinerary. Generic city pages that use a nearby airport,
or itineraries beginning with a rail connection, do not qualify. Service labels
such as scheduled, seasonal and charter are deliberately ignored.

--- ASIDE ---
Basel Airport (EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg) has flights displayed by
Google Flights, but never appeared in this 17-entry list. Google addresses it
in Saint-Louis, France, so it falls outside the Switzerland Places sweep by
geography. That is consistent with this project's map-boundary rule.
