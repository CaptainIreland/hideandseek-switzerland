=== AIRPORT COMMERCIAL-SERVICE AUDIT ===

Manual audit, 2026-08-03. Cross-checked each of the 17 review-count-proxy
airport entries (MIN_REVIEWS["airport"]=100 in scripts/filter_places.py)
against Google Flights (scheduled routes shown to/from the airport) and
Google Maps, corroborated independently via web search where Google Flights
alone was ambiguous between charter and scheduled service. This is a
one-time manual/web pass, not a reproducible script sweep - there is no
Google Flights API in this pipeline - so a future re-check means repeating
this by hand against the fresh candidate list, not re-running a script.

Airports checked: 17
Commercial (kept): 3
Not commercial (removed): 14

--- COMMERCIAL: scheduled flights confirmed ---
  - Geneva Airport - major international hub, scheduled flights year-round.
  - Zurich Airport - major international hub, scheduled flights year-round.
  - St. Gallen-Altenrhein Airport - Peoples Viennaline, about 11x/week
    nonstop to Vienna, year-round scheduled service.

--- NOT COMMERCIAL: removed ---
  - Air Ballonteam Stefan Zeberli GmbH - a hot-air-balloon touring company
    near St. Margrethen, not an airfield at all (no runway); Google Flights
    has no results for this location.
  - Airfield Langenthal - small grass strip ("Flugplatz Bleienbach"),
    general aviation, no scheduled service.
  - Aerodrome Epagny - "Aerodrome de la Gruyere", general aviation/gliding,
    no scheduled service.
  - Bern Airport - only seasonal Helvetic/Sky Alps Mediterranean holiday
    charter flights (Heraklion, Kos, Rhodes, Palma, Cagliari, Olbia) since
    SkyWork Airlines (its last scheduled carrier) collapsed in 2018; multiple
    aviation sources describe current traffic as "seasonal and charter"
    only, and Google Flights shows no bookable flight, just driving-time/
    hotel fallbacks.
  - Birrfeld Airport - general aviation/flight school, no scheduled service.
  - Emmen Airport - Militaerflugplatz Emmen, a Swiss Air Force base, no
    civilian scheduled service.
  - Engadin Airport (Samedan) - high-altitude general-aviation/glacier-
    flight strip; Google Flights recognises the place but redirects to
    Bergamo (BGY) or St. Gallen-Altenrhein (ACH) instead of listing any
    flight of its own.
  - Flugplatz Speck Fehraltorf - small grass strip, general aviation;
    Google Flights only treats "Fehraltorf" as a municipality and suggests
    nearby Zurich Airport instead.
  - Flugplatz Wangen-Lachen - lake-shore general-aviation strip, no
    scheduled service.
  - Grenchen Airport - general aviation/flight school, no scheduled service.
  - Lausanne Airport (La Blecherette) - general aviation airfield, no
    scheduled service.
  - Lugano Airport - lost its last scheduled carrier when Swiss/Adria's
    Etihad Regional service ended (Swiss International Air Lines ceased
    operations there in September 2019); now general and business aviation
    only. Google Flights recognises the place but redirects to Milan
    Malpensa (MXP) instead of listing a LUG flight.
  - Luzern-Beromuenster Airport - general aviation airfield, no scheduled
    service.
  - Sion Airport - primarily a Swiss Air Force fighter base, with civil
    aviation as a secondary activity; Helvetic's once-weekly seasonal Palma
    de Mallorca flight (May-October) is described by aviation sources as an
    "occasional charter", not regular scheduled service, and Google Flights
    shows no bookable flight for it, only driving-time/hotel fallbacks.

--- METHOD ---
For each airport: checked Google Flights (searching by name and/or nearest
IATA/ICAO code) and cross-referenced the pin on Google Maps to confirm it
was the same place, then independently corroborated via web search (airline
route pages, aviation-industry sources, Wikipedia) where Google Flights
alone left it ambiguous whether traffic was scheduled or seasonal/charter
(this applied to Bern and Sion in particular, both of which have real but
non-scheduled leisure-charter activity that could otherwise look like a
false negative).

--- ASIDE ---
Basel Airport (EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg) has genuine year-round
scheduled service alongside Zurich and Geneva, but never appeared in this
17-entry list at all - Google addresses it in Saint-Louis, France, so it
falls outside the Switzerland places sweep by geography, not by the review
threshold. Not a bug in this list; noted for completeness.
